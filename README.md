Reg X Timing provides professional race registration and timing services for events ranging from 5Ks to full triathlons.

## What We Offer

- Secure online race registration with instant confirmations
- Chip-based timing with live updates and split tracking
- Real-time results and leaderboards with downloadable reports
- End-to-end race management support for organizers
